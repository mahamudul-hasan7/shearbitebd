1. Problem Identification: What Problem Are We Solving?

Stating the core problem clearly in simple terms and why it is critically important to solve

THE PLAIN TRUTH
Food Is Dumped While People Sleep Hungry.

In Dhaka City, food waste is NOT caused by food shortage.

It is fundamentally an INFORMATION, COORDINATION, and MICRO-LOGISTICS failure.

Kitchens prepare in bulk. When trays remain at night, donors lack a rapid, verified channel to reach hungry communities within the critical biological safety window.

01 THE DAILY MIDNIGHT FOOD DUMP

University cafeterias (e.g. UIU Central Cafeteria) and restaurants cook in bulk. At 10 PM, 40–80 kg of edible food is thrown into dumpsters simply because donors lack instant charity contacts.

02 THE 4-HOUR BIOLOGICAL SAFETY CLOCK

Cooked food enters the bacterial danger zone (5°C–60°C). Under BFSA guidelines, it must be eaten within 4 hours. Manual phone calls take 5+ hours, resulting in complete food spoilage.

03 THE MISSING MICRO-LOGISTICS LINK

Local shelters and orphanages have zero standby vehicles, fuel budget, or couriers at night. Food is available 2 km away, but no automated software dispatches riders to deliver it.

💡 SUMMARY: Food exists and hunger exists within 3 km, but lack an automated software platform to connect them.

2. Main Features: 60-Sec Post & Proximity Radar Map

What will the end system be able to perform: Rapid listing, AI spoilage detection, and geo-filtering

FEATURE 01
60-Sec Listing & AI Spoilage Meter

• What it does: Cafeteria staff simply enters dish name, quantity, and cooking timestamp in 3 clicks.

• AI Spoilage Gauge: Computes real-time biological countdown based on ambient temperature and BFSA safe holding rules.

• Automated Meal Calculator: Automatically converts raw kilograms into child servings (e.g., '40 kg Biryani = 80–100 meals').

• Safety Timeout Lock: Automatically de-lists food if remaining pickup window drops below 45 minutes to guarantee safety.

FEATURE 02
3–5 km Hyper-Local Radar Map

• What it does: Interactive GIS radar map displaying available surplus batches in real-time.

• Traffic-Proof Proximity: Strictly prioritizes donations within a 3–5 km radius to bypass Dhaka's gridlock.

• 1-Click Rescue Claim: Verified NGO centers can lock and claim a food batch with a single tap.

• Urgency Rings: Food lots with under 2 hours remaining flash with pulsating red rings as 'Critical Urgency'.

⚡ OUTPUT: A donor posts surplus in under 60 seconds; nearby charities see it on the radar map instantly.

2. Main Features: Uber-Style Dispatch & OTP Handshake

What will the end system be able to perform: Crowdsourced student logistics and zero-trust verification

FEATURE 03
Uber-Style Volunteer Dispatch

• What it does: Micro-logistics dispatch engine powered by university cyclists and bikers.

• Smart Commute Alerts: Broadcasts push notifications to students heading home along the delivery corridor.

• Turn-by-Turn Navigation: Displays donor location, recipient shelter, and recommended cycling route.

• Thermal Carrier Compliance: In-app checklist prompts volunteers to verify insulated thermal bag hygiene.

FEATURE 04
4-Digit OTP & QR Handshake

• What it does: Cryptographic dual-layer verification at pickup and drop-off.

• Pickup Verification: Cafeteria donor only releases food when volunteer enters the secret 4-digit PIN (e.g. '7429').

• Drop-off Handover: Shelter representative scans dynamic QR token upon arrival to confirm delivery.

• Anti-Fraud Guarantee: 100% eliminates food diversion, black-market resale, or false delivery claims.

3. Plan of Action: Technology Selection & Rationale

What technologies will be used and why: Clear justification for frameworks and libraries

01
FRONTEND FRAMEWORK
Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS

What It Does: Unified web portal and mobile PWA for Donors, NGOs, and Volunteers.

Why Chosen:
• Sub-100ms load times over 4G cellular connections.
• Progressive Web App (PWA) eliminates app-store install barrier for student cyclists.
• Tailwind CSS v4 provides lightweight, responsive UI.

02
BACKEND & ORM
Prisma ORM + PostgreSQL + TypeScript

What It Does: Handles data persistence, auth & transactional claim state machine.

Why Chosen:
• ACID transactions prevent race conditions (two NGOs cannot double-claim the same batch).
• End-to-end type safety eliminates runtime schema errors.
• Relational integrity links Donors, NGOs, and Couriers.

03
GEOSPATIAL & MAPS
Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap (OSM)

What It Does: Renders interactive radar maps, circular radius queries & distance.

Why Chosen:
• 100% open-source without Google Maps billing or credit card dependencies.
• Lightweight client-side Haversine circular radius queries.
• Low battery and memory consumption on mobile devices.

💡 RATIONALE SUMMARY: Zero recurring cloud fees, strict type-safety, and instantaneous mobile performance.
