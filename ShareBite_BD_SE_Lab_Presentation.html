Section 1: Problem Identification

1. Problem Identification: What Problem Are We Solving?

The critical paradox of urban food waste vs. street hunger in Dhaka City

🔴 The Daily Food Dump

Every night, university cafeterias (like UIU), caterers & restaurants throw massive pots of safe, cooked meals into city dumpsters due to zero instant charity links.

⏳ The 4-Hour Biological Clock

Cooked food enters the bacterial danger zone (5°C–60°C). Under BFSA guidelines, food spoils before slow manual phone calls can coordinate a pickup.

🚫 The Missing Logistics Link

Charities lack standby transport at 10 PM. Food exists, hungry people are 2 km away, but zero automated micro-logistics platform connects them in time.

2. Solution: The Core Idea & System Approach

A synchronized 3-sided micro-logistics platform engineered for 15–30 minute rescue velocity

🌟 ONE SINGLE LINE (For a kid or parent):
"An Uber-like app where university cafeterias post extra hot food, and nearby charities and student volunteers get alerted to pick it up and feed hungry people before it gets cold and goes bad!"
The 3-Sided Hyper-Local Ecosystem:

🍲 1. Food Donors

Cafeterias & Caterers

• 60-Sec listing form
• AI Spoilage & Safety Meter
• Automated meal quantity calculator

🏢 2. Verified NGOs

Charities & Shelters

• Proximity Radar Map (3–5 km)
• 1-Click rescue claim
• Digital QR delivery verification

🚴 3. Student Volunteers

Campus Biker Squad

• Uber-style instant dispatch
• Turn-by-turn route navigation
• Insulated thermal carrier check

Section 2: Main Features & Outputs

2. Main Features: What Will the End System Perform?

Idea-driven key functionalities solving real friction in food rescue

⚡ 1. 60-Sec Listing & AI Spoilage Meter

Live temperature gauge (>65°C safe holding) + countdown clock. Converts weight to child meals (e.g., '40 kg Biryani = 80–100 meals').

📍 2. Proximity Radar Map (3–5 km)

Filters donations strictly within short radius to bypass Dhaka traffic. Batches under 2 hours flash as '🚨 Urgent Rescues'.

🚴 3. Uber-Style Volunteer Dispatch

Crowdsources campus student cyclists and bikers on daily commutes with verified thermal bag checklists.

🔒 4. 4-Digit OTP & QR Code Anti-Fraud

Zero theft: Donors verify volunteer's 4-digit PIN before handover; shelter scans QR token on arrival.

Section 3: Plan of Action (Tech Overview)

3. Plan of Action: High-Level Technology Overview

Modern open-source stack chosen for velocity, type-safety, and zero hosting cost

💻 Frontend: Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4

Why: Server-Side Rendering (SSR) delivers sub-100ms load times and full mobile responsiveness without native app installation friction.

🗄️ Backend: Prisma ORM + PostgreSQL + TypeScript / Zod

Why: Strongly-typed relational data models (Users, Donations, Claims, Receipts) with atomic state transitions and zero runtime type errors.

🗺️ Geospatial Maps: Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap

Why: 100% free, open-source geospatial routing & Haversine distance calculations without requiring expensive Google Maps API billing.
