## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one wide landscape website hero photograph, 16:9. Primary request: an NGO distributing fresh packed meals respectfully to adult community members in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Match the established ShareBite BD photography: candid documentary realism, warm late-afternoon window light, forest-green aprons, cream walls, natural skin texture, stainless serving vessels and plain kraft food boxes. A Bangladeshi woman volunteer in a deep green apron and another adult volunteer pass a closed paper meal box to a smiling adult recipient at a clean community distribution table. Other adults quietly receive food in the background. Subjects and the handover sit near the center-right so a dark website overlay can cover the left; keep the central interaction usable in a narrow portrait crop as well. Human, dignified, hopeful, understated, realistic hands and food handling. No children, no pity imagery, no brand logos, no lettering or signs, no text, no watermark. Color and visual style should match a warm bright volunteer community kitchen photo and an olive-green volunteer cyclist photograph, without being over-saturated. A single coherent full-bleed photographic scene, no montage or collage.
